# RL result figures

Generated from saved JSON artifacts by `scripts/plot_rl_result_insights.py`; no models were trained or rescored. PNG and SVG versions are in `docs/figures/rl_insights/`.

1. **Training budget** (`training_budget`): At 512 environment steps, PPO lagged random and evolutionary search on best frozen Ridge reward. At 4,096 steps, PPO improved markedly and roughly matched both search methods. Within each run, search was matched to PPO's unique training score budget. The two x-axis points have different compute budgets and are not a sample-efficiency curve. Bars are 95% Student-t intervals over five optimization seeds on one frozen predictor.
2. **Fresh starts** (`fresh_start_comparison`): On 32 new RL starting sequences, PPO with terminal reward and PPO with potential shaping beat random and evolutionary search at 16 proposal requests per start. This comparison matches inference requests only. PPO had substantial prior training; shaping used about 6.9 times more offline scoring requests than terminal PPO. The shaped-minus-terminal paired interval crosses zero. Starts came from the predictor's training pool, so they do not test unseen biological targets.
3. **Predictor cross-scoring** (`predictor_cross_scoring`): Five Ridge-trained and five GP-trained PPO policies made deterministic proposals on the same 32 starts. Paired GP-policy minus Ridge-policy rewards were evaluated by Ridge, GP and RF separately. All three 95% seed intervals cross zero. The RF evaluator was not used as a PPO reward. Scores from different evaluator rows should not be compared as common ground truth.

All three charts show **frozen surrogate engineering rewards**, not Pearson correlation, actual measured inhibition, target binding, safety or laboratory validation. GP and RF were the newer prediction models integrated from main; Ridge was the original RL reward. The ASO Atlas predictor study and its blocked ASO PPO gate are separate from these siRNA figures.
