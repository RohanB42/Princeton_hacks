# Scaling policy pretraining and PPO experience

Status: November 15, 2026 exploration and proposed protocol, not an executed
scaling experiment. This audit reads existing code, configuration, receipts and
already released diagnostic results. It does not fit models, query new molecules,
read final measured outcomes, change screening bounds or alter scientific profiles.

The immediate opportunity is broader training experience, rather than many more
repetitions of the present small candidate universe. The scientific predictors must
remain frozen during this policy experiment. Improving those predictors is a
separate study; changing both at once changes the reward surface and prevents
attributing an improvement to policy data scale.

Across all five independent policies, their combined pretraining/PPO datasets cover
1,575/1,602 compounds (98.31%). The five pretraining datasets alone cover 1,402.
This is an experiment-wide union, not data available to any one policy. It shows
why repeatedly collecting trajectories from these same windows has limited scope
to add molecular diversity. More epochs are also not more data: current pretraining
revisits 576 examples for 20 epochs, approximately 180 minibatch updates.

The often-mentioned approximately 2% fraction concerns the underlying efficacy
predictor's measured development cohort, not this policy-training universe. Policy
pretraining currently learns from frozen-surrogate pseudo-targets. PPO learns from
state/action/reward trajectories generated by that frozen environment. Neither is
a sample of 2% of the measured Atlas rows.

## Evidence for what to scale first

The pre-PPO staged policy equals or exceeds full PPO's mean eight-query utility on
five of six reported targets. The separately costed pretrained ranking control
also remains essential: it is a simpler use of the learned molecular prior and
cannot be dismissed merely because it lacks sequential RL. These comparisons are
in `results.json`, `summary.json` and `prior-control-results.json` under the v3
artifact directory and summarized in [the repair report](molecular-policy-repair.md).

Longer PPO is not monotonically better even on validation. Selected decision counts
for seeds 800–804 are 4,096 / 4,096 / 8,192 / 4,096 / 16,384. For example, seed 801's
six-campaign validation mean falls from 0.8830 at 4,096 to 0.7453 at 16,384. These
are development observations, not proof of a particular failure mechanism; possible
over-specialization or forgetting requires an ablation rather than an assertion.

The present `train()` checkpoint selector does not include the saved pre-PPO policy
as a selectable zero-step checkpoint. A new study should evaluate that checkpoint
under the same development protocol and retain it as a valid deployment choice.
Choosing only among policies that underwent PPO cannot demonstrate that PPO helped.

All currently reported targets, including F9, F12 and TTR, have now been inspected.
They are development evidence for subsequent tuning, not untouched confirmation.
The F12 candidate universe is entirely ineligible under the frozen diagnostic
screen; additional PPO cannot find an eligible design within that unchanged space.
Do not remove that target or relax the screen after observing its zero result.

## First study: diversity and pretraining before more PPO

Keep the current frozen model bundle, contexts, normalizers, reward and diagnostic
chemistry profile fixed. Existing expanded-chemistry applicability remains
unqualified; a larger surrogate experiment does not close that scientific gate.
Do not introduce extra chemistry patterns until the separate measured-data audit
justifies their predictor support. Initially add **more 48-nt campaigns**, retaining
89 candidates per episode, rather than enumerating a whole transcript at once.

Construct nested metadata-selected training gene sets of 6, 12 and 24 genes,
conditional on a valid metadata/split audit. The six existing training genes can
anchor the smallest set; additional genes must not overlap development or reserved
confirmation groups. These counts are proposed tiers, not a claim that 24 targets
have already passed scientific qualification. Select three nonoverlapping windows
per gene using a frozen outcome-blind rule. Record transcript versions/sequence
hashes and exact compound/sequence-family memberships before querying.

| Tier | Training genes | Windows | Queries per window, including start | Total pretraining queries per seed | Purpose |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| A0 | 6 | 18 | 32 | 576 | Current-scale reference |
| A1 | 12 | 36 | 16 | 576 | More genes at equal query cost |
| A2 | 24 | 72 | 8 | 576 | Broader coverage at equal query cost |
| A3 | 24 | 72 | 32 | 2,304 | More examples at fixed broad coverage |
| A4 | 24 | 72 | 89 | 6,408 | Entire broad training catalog, costed in full |

Start with A0–A3; A4 is a declared upper-bound training-data control if resources
and development findings justify proceeding. Uniform per-window candidate
permutations should be shared within seed, so deeper tiers contain shallower
samples. Deduplicate compounds across windows and count both unique physical
examples and every logical query; overlapping windows cannot manufacture extra
independent data. Use the same fixed starts across corresponding campaigns.

Evaluate both the staged pretrained policy and complete-candidate prior ranking,
plus unchanged random/greedy and the spec-required conventional controls. A primary
pretraining-data contrast can be A2 minus A0 at equal 576-query cost. A3 minus A2
is a secondary sample-count contrast. Neither is a PPO result.

The current pretrainer accepts epochs, so increasing sample count also increases
optimizer updates. Before claiming a pure data effect, add a step-budget interface.
A concrete compute-matched proposal is common update checkpoints 128/512/2,048,
batch size 64, with the same validation opportunities for every tier. This is a
predeclared resource schedule, not a convergence guarantee. It allows the broadest
catalog roughly 20 passes at the largest checkpoint while permitting early
validation selection for smaller datasets. Keep a fixed-20-epoch sensitivity
comparison if practical scaling under the current training recipe is also desired;
label its joint increase in data and optimizer compute explicitly.

Use five fresh development seeds for this engineering selection phase, with seeds
fixed before outcomes. Repeatedly increasing seeds until a preferred comparison
passes a significance threshold is not an acceptable stopping rule.

## Second study: does PPO add value at the better data scale?

After Stage A, freeze the chosen broad training set and pretraining recipe. A small
attribution matrix is sufficient initially:

| Factor | Levels |
| --- | --- |
| Pretraining count on the same 24-gene set | 576 and 2,304 |
| PPO decisions from that identical initialized policy | 0, 4,096, 16,384 |
| Development seeds | 5 paired seeds |

One 16,384-decision trajectory can save fixed 4,096 and 16,384 snapshots, reducing
this to ten PPO training runs rather than thirty independently retrained policies.
The zero-step snapshot is the exact same initialization. Evaluate fixed-prefix
snapshots for the training-scale curve; report validation-selected deployment
checkpoints separately so a larger budget is not credited merely for more chances
to select a lucky checkpoint. Preserve optimizer/RNG/state continuity and the
existing exact-resume invariants.

Every cell retains both staged no-PPO and full-budget ranking controls. Add a
training-query-cost comparison: spend additional permitted training queries on
pretraining-only data, up to the available training catalog, rather than assuming
PPO's extra calls are free. A rule matching the paired PPO run's observed query
expenditure must be declared before outcomes; if the finite training pool is
exhausted, report that saturation instead of inventing new unique examples.

Keep deployment requests fixed at eight including the common start. Thirty-two
requests remain a secondary extrapolation test while training episodes use eight.
A later 8/16/32 training-horizon curriculum is a distinct factor, with its own
versioned protocol, token reservations and ledger checks. Do not silently blend it
into the data-count comparison. New episodes currently sample campaigns uniformly;
with unequal windows per gene, implement gene-balanced then region-balanced
sampling to avoid accidentally overweighting genes with more windows.

Do not schedule 65,536 PPO decisions merely because they are available. Proceed to
that tier only if the 16,384-decision development curve shows a meaningful advantage
over both no-PPO controls or a clearly improving unresolved trajectory. If the
larger pretrainer wins and PPO adds little, retain the simpler model as the result.

## Cost and implementation constraints

Current full training receipts report 943.36–962.41 seconds per seed (15.7–16.0
minutes), including pretraining and checkpoint validation, under the executed CPU
runtime and concurrent load. These are per-run elapsed times, not independent
machine CPU-hours or the wall time of the whole parallel batch. At unchanged input
sizes, ten such runs imply approximately 2.6–2.7 aggregate run-hours; 65,536 decisions
would be roughly an hour per run under a simple linear extrapolation. These are
planning estimates, not benchmarks for the expanded target set. Pretraining-only
time is not separately instrumented, so it cannot be inferred from total run time.

The observed environment has site capacity 3,637, candidate capacity 89, archive
capacity 32 and 3,686 categorical actions. Its float32 observation arrays total
248,120 bytes. A 256-step buffer therefore holds approximately **60.6 MiB of
observation data alone**, before action masks stored separately, gradients,
optimizer state, activations and minibatch copies. Candidate features account for
27.8 MiB and full-target features for 24.9 MiB of that total.

Candidate storage grows as `4 × C × maximum_length × 16 × rollout_steps` bytes.
At 30,000 candidates, maximum length 20 and 256 steps, candidate features alone
would require approximately 9.16 GiB. That is why more small campaigns are the
initial scalable intervention. The current implementation materializes and copies
the whole catalog per observation and scores every candidate during policy forward
passes. Merely increasing `candidate_capacity` is not a scalable full-transcript
solution. Immutable catalog references/custom rollout storage or tested chunked
candidate scoring would require a separate schema and exact-probability tests.

More genes can still increase the longest transcript and therefore the padded
full-target CNN, action-mask size and memory. Set capacities from frozen metadata
before training, report excluded transcript lengths, and never truncate sequences
silently. Molecular scoring shares parameters across sites, but the global encoder
still receives position information; full translation invariance is not established.

Pretraining currently stacks all examples in memory: 2,304 examples ×20×16×4 is
approximately 2.81 MiB for the raw feature tensor, and 6,408 examples require about
7.82 MiB, before intermediates. This part is much cheaper to expand than the PPO
rollout catalog. Cache label-free target/pattern encodings by immutable campaign
identity where useful; do not expose unqueried scientific-head predictions.

Required infrastructure before execution is limited but concrete: parameterized
metadata-only campaign generation; nested training-query manifests; per-gene and
unique-compound coverage ledgers; pretraining update budgets and zero-step
validation; fixed-prefix PPO snapshots; stage-specific timers; separate logical
queries, unique physical evaluations and per-head costs; and target/family-aware
analysis. Shared caches may save physical computation, but every arm retains its
declared logical query charges and isolated search history.

## Confirmation and statistics

Use all inspected v3 targets only for development. Reserve fresh target/sequence-
family groups before further tuning, and audit exposure separately for policy
training and every scientific predictor. A target absent from PPO training is not
necessarily absent from the supervised efficacy/risk heads. Outcome-blind sequence
and metadata access for splitting must be declared; final outcomes remain sealed.

The canonical spec requires one primary contrast per experiment: the locked
pipeline versus the strongest conventional baseline selected on development data,
at the fixed deployment budget. PPO versus the strongest pretrained-only control
is a separate attribution contrast, either in a separately powered study or a
predeclared multiplicity-controlled family. Random and greedy remain visible even
if neither is the selected primary comparator. Exhaustive enumeration is a costed
89-request ceiling where feasible, not a free eight-request competitor.

Average multiple regions within each target/family before forming cross-target
claims. The same trained policy is used on many targets, so target and policy-seed
dependence are crossed. A target/family-and-seed cluster bootstrap should resample
seed indices consistently across targets, rather than treating all trajectories as
independent or independently resampling a different seed identity within each
target. Report target uncertainty and seed variability separately. Boundaries and
zero-inflated outcomes warrant sensitivity checks; five seeds cannot justify a
strong distributional assumption solely because a plotted difference looks large.
The general few-run concern and interval-based RL evaluation are documented by
[Agarwal et al., NeurIPS 2021](https://papers.nips.cc/paper/2021/hash/f514cec81cb148559cf475e7426eed5e-Abstract.html).

Plan sample size around a predeclared useful utility improvement, for example
0.03 at eight requests as a proposed computational threshold, not a biological
effect threshold. Use development-only variance estimates with conservative
sensitivity ranges for target heterogeneity, shared-policy correlation and
infeasible-target frequency. A starting design to assess is 20 fresh target/family
groups with ten policy seeds and three regions per group; those counts are a power
planning scenario, not a guarantee or an already-qualified cohort. Increase target
groups before merely adding seeds on the same few genes. Freeze the final count
before evaluating confirmation outcomes, and do not continue until significance.

For scale awareness, 20 targets ×3 regions ×10 seeds ×6 methods costs 28,800 scoring
requests at the eight-request cap. Adding the 32-request secondary cap raises this
to 144,000 requests, or 288,000 calls with the present two-head scorer, excluding
training and other reference evaluations. That is a reason to stage development
before confirmation, not to reduce or conceal the cost denominator.

Go forward with broader pretraining if it improves held-out development utility at
equal cost or yields a useful quality/cost tradeoff without degrading coverage.
Go forward with larger PPO only if it adds value beyond the strongest no-PPO
control. Stop a run on integrity, partition, accounting or numerical failure;
retain unfavorable but valid results. A frozen-oracle improvement remains a
computational diagnostic until separate predictor applicability and independent
measured evidence requirements are met.

## Audit performed

Read-only inspection covered the required project guidance, final molecular spec,
`candidate_policy.py`, `candidate_encoding.py`, `pretraining.py`, `training.py`,
`gym_env.py`, `policy.py`, the v3 runner and saved protocol/receipts/results. Unique
coverage and validation trajectories were recomputed from existing journals.
Observation dimensions were inspected by constructing a Gym wrapper; its core
remained unset, so this performed no predictor query. No new training, molecule
scoring, final-label read, data edit or shared-code modification was performed.
