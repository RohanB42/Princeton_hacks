## Current direction

The proposed core is chemistry-constrained ASO molecular design using a frozen,
interpretable reward adapter over applicable endpoint predictors. Compare
MaskablePPO with random valid search, greedy/hill climbing and genetic optimization
under matched scoring budgets. Evaluate surrogate exploitation and independent
evidence, not just the optimizer's own reward.

The synthetic-data hypothesis is whether constrained, cautiously pseudo-labeled
augmentation improves reward-model robustness and independently evaluated designs
without more experimental labels. Planned controls are real-only models (R0),
augmented models (R1), and simpler regularization/uncertainty safeguards. Synthetic
labels are estimates, not measurements. This study has not established RL benefits,
clinical safety, therapeutic improvement or experimental label savings.

RNA-FM/LoRA is a supporting predictor branch. It can eventually supply an endpoint
head; it is not a prerequisite for the gym or a validated unified efficacy/safety
model. Keep siRNA efficacy separate from ASO efficacy and chemistry-specific risks.

## Methodological caveats to retain

1. Source validation membership changes across budgets. Total acquired 3%/5%
   cohorts are nested, but inner fitting cohorts are not strictly nested because
   patent-group validation is recomputed. Seed 512 has 56 fitting target
   identifiers at 3% versus 53 at 5%. The data-size contrast therefore includes
   changing group composition; it does not isolate row count alone. Keep the
   frozen study and disclose this limitation. Within-budget source-checkpoint
   comparisons retain matched fitting rows.
2. Raw source-validation RMSEs across 3% and 5% are not a common-test learning
   curve. Compare source sizes on identical development-target evaluation rows
   after matched adaptation, while retaining the composition caveat above.
3. Fixed-three-epoch target fits use all 200 acquired labels. Duration-selected
   fits use 160–166 gradient rows plus 34–40 validation rows. Their contrast changes
   both checkpoint selection and gradient-row count, not just training duration.
4. Source validation is patent-held-out, not strictly compound-held-out. Three of
   six partitions have one exact normalized compound crossing fit/validation;
   natural-base identity overlaps range from zero to four. Source-to-target
   exclusions remain enforced. Patent identifiers are not verified patent families.
5. The initial profile loaded the cohort before restricting labels. Later execution
   filters endpoint reads at the Parquet boundary. Reserved outcomes were not used
   for fitting, stopping or analysis. Original invocation sources/hashes are
   preserved locally; the running process predates later audit/config refinements.
6. The runtime borrows local environments and uses local checkpoint paths. A clean,
   portable installation is not established. Generated artifacts and weights stay
   ignored. Source identifiers are not fully resolved canonical gene aliases.

## Implications for the proposed RL gym

- Make endpoint predictors interchangeable. Classical models remain the reference;
  LoRA is an alternative to evaluate, not an assumed improvement.
- Select predictor checkpoints with training-internal validation and freeze them
  before RL. Do not use final evaluation outcomes for predictor or PPO selection.
- Keep an independent final evaluation. Use strictly held-out measured outcomes
  where available; separate predictor rankings for novel designs are supporting
  computational evidence, not biological validation. A pseudo-labeling teacher
  cannot be the sole independent evaluator of its own augmented reward model.
- Compare PPO with simple search under matched total scoring budgets, including
  policy-training costs. Measure validity, diversity, OOD drift, reward components
  and the gap between optimized reward and independent evaluation.
- Preserve chemistry, assay and target-recognition applicability. Inapplicable
  safety endpoints remain unknown. These efficacy fits do not validate a unified
  multi-endpoint reward or demonstrate resistance to reward hacking.
- For R0/R1 comparisons, match real labels, architecture and tuning effort, and
  include a simpler regularization control before crediting synthetic augmentation.

These are methodological decisions/recommendations for the new gym, not claims
that the complete ASO PPO and external-evaluation system is already implemented.
