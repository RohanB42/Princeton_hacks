## Executive assessment

We have built a functioning retrospective active-learning benchmark, reproduced several published efficacy baselines, completed multiple acquisition studies, evaluated frozen and adapted RNA foundation-model representations, and integrated a separate biological candidate-ranking application.

The strongest current acquisition finding is a **small improvement in average held-out learning-curve performance** for uncertainty sampling and a hybrid policy in a reported 40-seed Huesken/GP study. **Reliable, substantial savings in experimental labels have not been established.** Our separate five-seed GP development study is mixed, and the earlier CNN diversity pilot was negative. These are different protocols and should not be pooled.

The RNA-FM branch produced useful negative findings: frozen representations did not improve low-label prediction or acquisition geometry consistently. Actual LoRA adaptation improved its matched frozen neural-head control, but lost to tuned one-hot Ridge in all five seeds on the primary learning-curve statistic. The current setup does not justify making RNA-FM the primary predictor.

The expanded Gate 1 goal—published winning models on all seven efficacy datasets—is **not fully reproduced**. Four of seven local means fall within the numerical ±0.03 tolerance; historical split and execution provenance remain incomplete. A separate two-model Huesken reassessment described Gate 1 as closed within its narrower scope. That does not close the expanded seven-dataset requirement.

## 1. Scientific objective and boundaries

The main question remains whether choosing which oligos to measure can reach comparable predictive performance with fewer **real experimental labels** than random selection. Each campaign fits a predictor on revealed labels, selects eligible pool members, reveals their existing assay measurements, retrains, and evaluates at prescribed checkpoints. This is retrospective replay, not a new wet-lab campaign.

The primary endpoint is Huesken siRNA reporter-assay percentage inhibition. Prediction, experimental selection, and molecular design are separate tasks. ASO neurotoxicity and immunomodulation endpoints remain separate from siRNA efficacy; they cannot be combined into a validated per-molecule “safe and effective” claim.

Our prospective contribution is a reproducible evaluation of selection methods and representations on oligo data. We have not invented active learning, established exclusivity, demonstrated better therapeutic molecules, or shown superiority to a proprietary platform such as Creyon.

## 4. Active-learning experiments completed

Normalized Pearson AULC is the area under performance versus label count divided by the budget interval. It measures average performance across that interval; it is not a percentage of labels saved. Unless explicitly labeled a confidence interval, ± below denotes sample standard deviation across seeds.

### Cross-dataset engineering diagnostic

Completed **180 campaigns**: 120 exact-HELM group-holdout campaigns across all 12 datasets and 60 target-group campaigns across six supported datasets. These used a generic HELM-hash RF representation and random versus tree-spread uncertainty. Uncertainty improved AULC on five of 12 sequence tasks and worsened it on seven. This established broad pipeline coverage, not benchmark comparability or consistent acquisition benefit. [Protocol](../full-experiment-protocol.md).

### CNN diversity pilot: seeds 0–4

Ten campaigns compared random selection with native k-mer diversity at 50–500 labels using the source-pinned CNN, plus five fresh full-training-pool references.

- Diversity minus random AULC: **−0.014966 ± 0.039759**; positive in two of five seeds.
- At 500 labels: random **r = 0.537811**, diversity **0.428464**, full-pool reference **0.613164**.
- The reference-minus-0.03 threshold was reached by random in zero of five and diversity in one of five campaigns within the budget cap. No reliable savings ratio follows.
- All 30 target genes appeared on both sides of these five splits: this does not test unseen-gene acquisition.

The scientific audit checked ledgers, label boundaries and sequence overlap. Its raw-position Hamming audit is not equivalent to ruling out every shifted alignment or biological relationship. [Protocol](../huesken-learning-protocol.md), [audit](../scientific-audit-20261114-0545.md).

### GP development: seeds 200–204

Completed **20 campaigns** with random, one-hot diversity, GP latent uncertainty, and a hybrid selecting half the batch by uncertainty and half by coverage. Kernel selection used only the initial 50 acquired labels with internal validation. Budgets extended through 1,000, then 1,250, 1,500 and the full eligible pool; primary AULC used 50–1,000.

| Policy | Paired AULC difference vs random | Mean paired label savings to reference − 0.03 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Diversity | −0.007429 ± 0.028200 | +3.656% ± 27.191 percentage points |
| Uncertainty | +0.000676 ± 0.015544 | −4.056% ± 27.613 percentage points |
| Hybrid | +0.005352 ± 0.020786 | +22.737% ± 28.008 percentage points |

The full-pool reference averaged **0.625275 ± 0.026814**. Hybrid savings ranged from approximately **−14% to +46%**, including a zero-saving seed. Seed 202 showed highly unstable low-budget behavior before recovering at the full pool. It was retained; optimization/model-selection instability is a hypothesis requiring diagnosis, not an established root cause.

This is promising only as a small development signal. The proposed larger replication of this exact protocol has not been completed. [Protocol and results](../active-learning-development.md).

## 5. RNA foundation-model branch

Implemented verified RNA-FM loading, a provenance-aware embedding cache, representation interfaces, frozen-head studies, acquisition-space comparisons, actual low-rank adapters, and a bounded multi-seed adaptation study.

The encoder has **99,521,546 parameters**. Source revision is `348951516e0963d22bbb33b3c9fc18c89081d38e`; checkpoint SHA-256 is `5b5d7d87b37c291ef42c140ef9edf7aea29f255fa2a4fd435f776c52e93d5e99`. Each strand is encoded separately; layer-12 nucleotide embeddings are mean-pooled and concatenated into 1,280 features. This omits chemistry and does not infer which source strand is the guide. No silent fallback features are accepted. A randomly initialized encoder provides a separate control.

### Actual LoRA adaptation and final comparison

The initial 50-label proof verified real adapter updates with unchanged base weights. It used rank-4 Q/V adapters across 24 projections; weight parametrization was necessary because fused attention can bypass ordinary module forward hooks. A single favorable seed established feasibility, not efficacy.

The completed comparison used seeds **400–404**, matched random budgets **50/100/200**, four arms, **60 checkpoint fits**, and ten separate classical full-pool references. Neural arms selected three or ten epochs using acquired-label internal validation, then refit from fresh initialization. No larger-budget adapter was reused at a smaller budget.

| Model | r at 50 | r at 100 | r at 200 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| One-hot + tuned Ridge | 0.3131 | 0.3805 | **0.4910** |
| Frozen RNA-FM + tuned Ridge | 0.1906 | 0.2723 | 0.3374 |
| Frozen RNA-FM + neural head | 0.1548 | 0.1544 | 0.2801 |
| LoRA RNA-FM + matched neural head | 0.1869 | 0.1939 | **0.4196** |

- Primary LoRA minus one-hot AULC: **−0.13814 ± 0.04807**, negative in **5/5** seeds.
- LoRA minus matched frozen neural head: **+0.07159 ± 0.02762**, positive in **5/5** seeds.
- LoRA minus frozen Ridge: **−0.01239 ± 0.05769**, mixed across seeds.
- No arm reached the shared **r = 0.60** threshold by 200 labels. No label-savings ratio was established.
- All 15 final LoRA fits verified changed adapters and unchanged base weights. Runtime was **26.2 minutes**, peak process RSS **2.06 GiB**. Cached versus neural frozen-feature parity was exact on the checked rows.

**Recommendation:** park larger sweeps of this setup and prioritize core acquisition. Alternative pooling, schedules and larger budgets remain hypotheses. Pretraining sequence overlap has not been fully audited; no downstream Huesken-trained checkpoint was used.

## 8. Next decisions and work

1. **Reconcile the evidence before strengthening claims.** Audit the incoming study's predictor-selection provenance and threshold censoring, diagnose seed 202 in the separate GP study, and keep the two protocols distinct. Resolve outstanding Gate 1 discrepancies or explicitly delimit the reproducibility claim.
2. **Test stronger batch selection before RL.** MaxDet and integrated variance reduction/BAIT were discussed as next comparisons; they are **not implemented or run yet**. Compare against random and the strongest existing simple policy with a fixed predictor and matched budgets. Any reference distribution for acquisition must exclude held-out evaluation information.
3. **Make label savings a direct endpoint.** Freeze an achievable threshold, crossing rule and censoring treatment before new outcomes. Report paired uncertainty, failures to reach the threshold, and learning-curve area without substituting the latter for savings.
4. **Test generalization.** Extend acquisition to held-out targets and another compatible efficacy dataset. Additional seeds on Huesken improve estimation within that setting but do not establish transfer to new assays or genes.
5. **Keep RNA-FM bounded.** Preserve its negative findings and working adaptation infrastructure. Resume only for a specific new hypothesis rather than scaling the same unfavorable setup.
6. **Treat RL as a later research extension.** A controller could learn when to choose exploration, coverage or exploitation, but no learned acquisition policy has been trained here. Rewards must use training-internal information, and training episodes/tasks must be separated from evaluation. Compare with simple schedules and stronger batch baselines first.
7. **Finish reproducibility packaging.** Integrate the final LoRA report, document artifact regeneration and environment installation, verify clean-install execution, and align outdated status language across project documents.

**Current defensible conclusion:** the system works as an experimental harness and provides evidence of a modest, protocol-dependent acquisition benefit. The central practical promise—substantial, reproducible savings in measured labels—remains an open research question.
